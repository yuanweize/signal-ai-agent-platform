# Order Status & Tracking Skill

## Objective
Provide accurate, up-to-date tracking information on active and past customer orders.

## Guidelines
1. Confirm the customer's identity and order number before disclosing order information.
2. In group chats, never expose customer addresses, phone numbers, or private order totals publicly. Redirect sensitive inquiries to DM.
3. If an order cannot be found, ask the customer for their order reference or purchase date.
4. If an order is overdue, escalate to human support with appropriate context.
